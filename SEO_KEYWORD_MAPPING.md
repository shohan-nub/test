# SparkLab SEO Phase 1 | Keyword and search intent map

Research date **22 November 2026**. This document assigns search intent to the five existing public pages and identifies gaps for a later writing phase. All five pages returned HTTP 200 on the local production build. The URLs below are the configured public canonicals. This research includes no production ranking assessment.

## Business and market evidence

- **Positioning.** SparkLab describes itself as a creative technology studio that designs and builds useful, reliable digital products around how a business works. Its offer spans discovery, design, engineering and launch. The Services page publishes indicative USD project ranges and sends buyers to a service-specific inquiry. [Homepage](src/app/page.tsx) · [About content](src/app/(agency)/about/about-data.ts) · [Services page](src/app/(agency)/services/page.tsx)
- **Verified service lines.** Website design and development, including e-commerce and CMS integration. iOS and Android mobile app development. AI assistants, chatbot development and workflow automation. Technical and on-page SEO plus AI search visibility. Custom software, dashboards and inventory systems. Brand identity and 3D product visuals. UI/UX, Next.js work and API integrations are supporting capabilities. [Service catalog](src/app/services-data.ts)
- **Likely buyers.** Owners and founders looking to launch or rebuild a site or product, operations teams with recurring manual work, and marketing teams responsible for a storefront, brand or search visibility. Retail and e-commerce are plausible segments based on the e-commerce and inventory offers. The gallery provides examples of website, brand and digital product work, including a payment-themed identity and mobile interfaces. Confirm project ownership, sector experience, client permissions and published outcomes before making industry-specific claims. [Selected Work data](src/app/components/ProjectGalleryLayout.tsx)

English copy, USD estimates, remote booking and the homepage's cross-border positioning support testing English-language international demand alongside Bangladesh. The map illustrates connections and the FAQ mentions Singapore hosting. Those signals establish neither offices abroad nor client markets. ## Priority and decisions before writing

1. **Optimize Services first for “AI workflow automation services.”** The page already explains the offer, provides an indicative price and links to the relevant inquiry. The phrase matches the implemented service and the provider pages surfaced in search. This ordering is based on intent and fit, with demand still to be validated.
2. **Close the proof and scope gaps first.** Prioritize a publishable automation example with the original manual task, inputs and tools, what SparkLab delivered and how it was assessed. Add specific deliverables, dependencies and price drivers. Report results only where documented and approved. Disambiguate marketing websites versus internal software, and workflow automation versus SEO and AI search visibility.
3. **Confirm market and delivery assumptions.** Establish priority buyer type, markets and languages, whether Bangladesh or cross-border work should lead, which client industries have approved evidence, what integrations the team supports, current budgets and engagement models, and what handover, hosting or ongoing support actually includes. Confirm how “SparkLab” and “Spark Lab” should be used as brand variants.
4. **Treat overlap as a planning risk.** The homepage and Services share multiple commercial topics, and About and Approach both describe process. The map assigns a primary intent to each. Actual cannibalization would require evidence that multiple URLs surface for the same relevant queries, such as Search Console query-by-page data after launch. Contact should remain the clear destination for branded inquiries and bookings.
