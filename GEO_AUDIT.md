# SparkLab Generative Engine Optimization audit

**Audit date** · 22 November 2026  
**Scope** · ChatGPT Search, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Gemini Apps, Microsoft Copilot, and Perplexity. Reviewed the five public routes in the local production build, the current source, the three SEO reports, public branded searches, and platform documentation. [Keyword map](SEO_KEYWORD_MAPPING.md) · [Services brief](SEO_SERVICES_PAGE_BRIEF.md) · [Services facts](SEO_SERVICES_FACTS.md)

## Executive summary

- **Verified** · The local build serves all five public pages with their main content in HTML, descriptive titles, sitewide internal links, and production canonicals. Its sitemap lists those pages and its wildcard robots policy allows them.
- **Partially verified** · SparkLab's core identity is clear in the site's text. The approved spelling is **SparkLab**, while several shared titles and social fields use **Spark Lab**. Copy and assets support the Bangladesh-based technology studio identity and worldwide service positioning.
- **Missing** · This audit found no attributable independent business profile, client automation case study, or verified AI citation for this studio. The permanent domain is prelaunch and could not be inspected through public browsing. Crawl behavior at the public edge, indexing, and AI visibility remain unverified.

**Status labels** · Verified means directly supported by source, rendered output, or an attributable public source. Partially verified means the evidence covers only part of the claim. Missing means no supporting evidence was found in the materials inspected. Requires business approval means the studio needs to confirm a fact, public use, or policy. Platform eligibility is distinct from actual indexing, ranking, recommendation, or citation.

## AI crawler accessibility

**Verified locally.** All five pages and both discovery endpoints returned HTTP 200. The parsed page responses showed one expected canonical each, and no public page had a `noindex` meta directive. The sitemap advertised the five canonical URLs. The robots policy has one wildcard group allowing `/` and excluding `/api/`. There are no named crawler overrides. The previous technical audit also checked 25 internal destinations and anchors. Source retains the preview-specific `noindex, nofollow` configuration and empty preview sitemap. [Robots route](src/app/robots.ts) · [Sitemap route](src/app/sitemap.ts) · [Canonical configuration](src/app/site-metadata.ts) · [Root layout](src/app/layout.tsx)

| ChatGPT Search | Verified | `OAI-SearchBot` is the search crawler and matches the public allow policy. A request using its user-agent name received HTTP 200 on local Services. `ChatGPT-User` is a user-triggered fetcher and has a different role from automatic Search crawling. [OpenAI crawler documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots) |

**Potential policy changes · Requires business approval.** The application presents no accidental search-crawler block. Decide separately whether to permit copying for training and whether to permit grounding. For example, a future GPTBot restriction can coexist with OAI-SearchBot access. A Google-Extended restriction has Gemini grounding implications under current Google documentation. Bing's current controls distinguish training from grounding. Review the production firewall and verified bot logs before adding custom crawl groups. [OpenAI controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots) · [Google controls](https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawlers-fetchers/google-common-crawlers) · [Bing controls](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/new-ai-preferences-2t1cc16d)

## Missing information and five priorities

1. **Missing · Verify public access after launch.** Check all canonical URLs, live robots and sitemap, HTTP headers, and whether the CDN permits authentic search crawlers. Verify Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools properties and submit the canonical sitemap. Establish a visibility baseline before interpreting referral or citation changes. [Google AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) · [Bing AI Performance](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/?topicid=9f8e7d6c)
2. **Requires business approval · Normalize the business identity.** Confirm the approved public spelling, any real alternate or legal name, approved service area, and which contact and location details belong in public profiles and metadata.
3. **Requires business approval · Verify external profiles.** Obtain owned profile URLs and permission to link them. Verify the provenance and measurement dates of platform statistics before relying on them. Check that confirmed profiles use the same brand and domain.
4. **Requires business approval · Document what buyers can verify.** Confirm supported client integrations, data handling, approval steps, project handover, support, and pricing inclusions. Gather one publishable automation case with scope and attributable evidence. Label internal demos and hypothetical workflows clearly. Add outcome figures only with measurement and approval.
5. **Partially verified · Prepare a minimal entity graph.** Review Organization and WebSite first, using verified information and consistent IDs. Consider Service only where the descriptions and scope are clear. Validate any future graph against visible content and keep speculative fields out. Recheck crawler policy with the owner before any change.
